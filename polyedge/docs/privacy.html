Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

1. Introduction

PolyEdge ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth).
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about how you use the app.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, app version for troubleshooting.
  • Payment Data: Processed entirely by Stripe. We never store full credit card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the app
  • To authenticate your account and secure your data
  • To process subscription payments via Stripe
  • To send service-related communications
  • To analyse usage patterns

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Auth and data storage. Privacy Policy
  • Stripe — Payment processing. Privacy Policy
  • Expo — Build infrastructure. Privacy Policy
  • Polymarket — Market data for predictions.
  • DeepSeek — AI-powered analysis.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers (EU region) with encryption in transit and at rest.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. Deletion requests fulfilled within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

Access, correct, delete, or export your data. Contact support@polyedge.app.

8. Children's Privacy

Not intended for users under 13.

9. Changes
