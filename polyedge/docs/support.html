FAQ

How do I create an account?

Open the PolyEdge app and tap "Sign In" on the Profile tab. You can register with your email address.

How does PolyEdge generate predictions?

Our AI engine analyzes on-chain data, smart money movement, market sentiment, and historical patterns to produce confidence-scored predictions.

Is this financial advice?

No. PolyEdge provides analytical insights and information only. All trading and investment decisions are your own responsibility.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Go to the Profile tab in the app and tap "Upgrade" to subscribe via Stripe for €9.99/month.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at support@polyedge.app with the subject "Delete Account" and we'll process your request within 48 hours.
