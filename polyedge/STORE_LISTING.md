## Full Description
PolyEdge brings institutional-grade prediction market analysis to your pocket. Our AI engine analyzes millions of data points from prediction markets to surface the highest-confidence opportunities.

**Key Features:**

• **Daily AI Picks** — Every morning, our intelligence engine analyzes market data, smart money movement, and confidence scoring to deliver curated picks with clear YES/NO recommendations.

• **Smart Money Tracking** — See what the top traders are doing. Track wallet-level positions, win rates, and ROI from the most sophisticated market participants.

• **Real-Time Market Insights** — Browse prediction markets with AI-generated briefs explaining the reasoning behind each recommendation.

• **Confidence Scoring** — Every pick comes with a confidence score (60-99%), AI reasoning, and key supporting factors so you can make informed decisions.

• **Whale Activity Monitor** — Track large position movements and follow the smartest money in the market.

• **No Financial Advice** — PolyEdge provides analytical insights only. All trading decisions are yours.

**Privacy & Security:**
- Anonymous account creation with email
- No sharing of your personal data
- Secure authentication via Supabase
