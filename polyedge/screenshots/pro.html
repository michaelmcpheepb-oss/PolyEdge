Frequently Asked Questions
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your PolyEdge Pro subscription at any time. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.
Is there a free trial?
Yes! The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged until the trial period ends.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay through our secure Stripe integration.
Do I need a Polymarket account to use PolyEdge?
No, PolyEdge works as a standalone companion app. However, to place trades you'll need a Polymarket account.
